# Musical Genres, Styles, Moods, and Multi-Dimensional Composition

Music classification is not one-dimensional. A song is defined across multiple orthogonal dimensions: **Genre**, **Subgenre / Style**, **Mood**, **Purpose**, **Instrumentation**, and **Vocal Delivery**. Combining these dimensions produces specific, authentic musical identities.

---

## 1. Major Music Genres

### 1. Pop
- **Core Attributes**: Catchy melodies, memorable hooks, diatonic chord progressions, polished production, mainstream appeal.
- **Typical BPM Range**: 110 – 128 BPM.
- **Common Subgenres & Examples**:
  - *Dance Pop*: High-energy four-on-the-floor beat, driving bass.
  - *Synth-Pop*: 80s analog synthesizers, punchy gated snares, clean hooks.
  - *Electropop*: Modern EDM-influenced pop with sidechained compression.
  - *Teen Pop*: Upbeat, melodic, youthful lyricism.
- **Instrumentation**: Electric piano, acoustic/electric guitars, synthesized bass, tight electronic drums, backing vocal stacks.

### 2. Hip-Hop / Rap
- **Core Attributes**: Rhythmic vocal cadence, heavy drum beats, syncopated 808 sub-bass, wordplay, storytelling, and flow.
- **Typical BPM Range**: 75 – 95 BPM (Boom Bap / Traditional), 130 – 160 BPM (Trap / Half-time).
- **Common Subgenres & Examples**:
  - *Trap*: Rapid 16th/32nd-note hi-hat rolls, deep 808 sub-bass, minimalist minor melodies.
  - *Drill*: Sliding 808 pitch glides, dark minor chord stabs, syncopated snares.
  - *Boom Bap*: Soul/jazz vinyl sampling, crunchy acoustic kick/snare swing.
  - *Conscious Rap*: Lyrical complexity, political/social themes, organic instrumentation.

### 3. Rock
- **Core Attributes**: Electric guitars with overdrive/distortion, acoustic drums, prominent basslines, raw dynamic vocal delivery.
- **Typical BPM Range**: 110 – 145 BPM.
- **Common Subgenres & Examples**:
  - *Classic Rock*: Riff-driven pentatonic grooves, tube amp distortion, driving rhythm section.
  - *Hard Rock*: Heavier power chords, blues-scale solos, aggressive chest voice.
  - *Punk Rock*: Fast tempo (150+ BPM), short 3-chord songs, raw DIY energy, anti-establishment lyrics.
  - *Alternative / Indie Rock*: Jangle guitars, unconventional song structures, introspective lyrics.

### 4. Electronic / EDM
- **Core Attributes**: Synthesizers, electronic drum machines, sound design, build-ups, dynamic filter sweeps, and drops.
- **Typical BPM Range**: 120 – 175 BPM (House: 124-128, Techno: 130-135, Drum & Bass: 170-175).
- **Common Subgenres & Examples**:
  - *House*: 4-on-the-floor kick, swung 16th hi-hats, piano stabs.
  - *Techno*: Hypnotic repetition, dark industrial textures, relentless kick drum.
  - *Trance*: Euphoric arpeggios, epic builds, melodic breakdowns.
  - *Dubstep*: Heavy syncopation, half-time wobble bass, sub-frequency drops.
  - *Drum and Bass*: Fast breakbeats (174 BPM), deep Reese basslines.
  - *Future Bass*: Detuned supersaws, pitch-bent vocal chops, expressive jazz chords.

### 5. Jazz
- **Core Attributes**: Improvisation, complex extended chords (7ths, 9ths, 11ths, 13ths), swung rhythms, expressive acoustic instruments.
- **Typical BPM Range**: 60 – 240+ BPM.
- **Common Subgenres & Examples**:
  - *Bebop*: Rapid tempos, virtuosic phrasing, asymmetrical lines.
  - *Swing*: Big band brass sections, walking acoustic bass, syncopated rhythm section.
  - *Smooth Jazz*: Polished production, melodic soprano saxophone, electric piano grooves.
  - *Fusion*: Jazz harmony merged with electric rock guitars, funk rhythms, and synthesizers.

### 6. Classical
- **Core Attributes**: Intricate acoustic arrangements, counterpoint, formal development, wide dynamic range (pianissimo to fortissimo).
- **Common Periods & Forms**:
  - *Baroque*: Harpsichord, strict counterpoint, fugues (Bach, Vivaldi).
  - *Romantic*: Emotional intensity, lush string orchestras, dramatic rubato (Chopin, Tchaikovsky).
  - *Symphony / Concerto*: Multi-movement orchestral forms.
  - *Opera*: Theatrical orchestral storytelling with classical operatic vocal registers.

### 7. Country / Folk
- **Core Attributes**: Storytelling, narrative-driven lyricism, acoustic guitars, cultural heritage, vocal twang.
- **Typical BPM Range**: 85 – 120 BPM.
- **Common Subgenres & Examples**:
  - *Bluegrass*: Fast acoustic picking, banjo, fiddle, mandolin, upright bass.
  - *Americana*: Blend of roots music, blues, and folk storytelling.
  - *Country Pop*: Contemporary crossover with pop hooks and acoustic instrumentation.
  - *Folk Rock*: Acoustic singer-songwriter lyricism backed by rock rhythm section.

### 8. Indian Music
- **Core Attributes**: Raga (melodic frameworks with microtonal shrutis), Tala (rhythmic cycles like Teental, Keherwa), emotional Rasa, ornamented vocal delivery (meend, gamak).
- **Typical BPM Range**: 70 – 130 BPM.
- **Common Traditions & Genres**:
  - *Hindustani Classical*: Northern classical tradition, sitar, sarod, tabla, khyal singing.
  - *Carnatic*: Southern classical tradition, veena, mridangam, precise mathematical rhythms.
  - *Bollywood*: Grand commercial fusion combining traditional ragas with modern pop/orchestral production.
  - *Qawwali*: Devotional Sufi music with harmonium, rhythmic handclaps, and ecstatic vocal crescendo.
  - *Ghazal*: Poetic, contemplative Urdu poetry set to gentle acoustic/harmonium arrangements.
  - *Bhangra*: High-energy Punjabi folk-pop with dhol drums, chimta, and celebratory shouting.

### 9. Reggae / Caribbean
- **Core Attributes**: Offbeat rhythmic accents (the "skank"), deep syncopated basslines, one-drop drum rhythms, roots philosophy.
- **Typical BPM Range**: 65 – 85 BPM.
- **Common Subgenres & Examples**:
  - *Roots Reggae*: Rastafarian themes, organic horn sections, philosophical lyrics.
  - *Dub*: Instrumental remixes emphasizing heavy bass, space echoes, tape delays, and drum drops.
  - *Dancehall*: Faster electronic riddims, energetic toasting/rapping.
  - *Ska*: Fast, energetic walking basslines with bright brass and guitar chops on every up-beat.

### 10. Metal
- **Core Attributes**: High-gain distorted guitars, double-bass drumming, complex time signatures, aggressive vocal techniques (growling, screaming, operatic belting).
- **Typical BPM Range**: 110 – 220+ BPM.
- **Common Subgenres & Examples**:
  - *Heavy Metal*: Twin guitar harmonies, galloping riffs, soaring vibrato vocals (Iron Maiden).
  - *Death Metal*: Down-tuned guitars, blast beats, chromatic guttural vocals.
  - *Metalcore*: Heavy breakdowns, alternating screamed verses and clean melodic choruses.
  - *Symphonic Metal*: Cinematic orchestral strings and operatic female vocals combined with heavy metal guitars (Nightwish).

## 3. Mood Classification

Mood reflects the emotional color of the song, independent of the genre:

1. **Happy**: Bright major scales, bouncy syncopation, uplifting lyrics, major triads.
2. **Sad**: Natural minor scale, slower tempos, sparse arrangement, descending melody lines.
3. **Romantic**: Warm analog pads, nylon guitars, intimate close-mic vocals, major 7th and 9th chords.
4. **Heartbroken**: Raw emotional vocal cracks, minor chords resolving to diminished tension, weeping guitar or cello.
5. **Energetic**: High tempo, driving eighth-note bassline, aggressive compression, bright transients.
6. **Motivational**: Rising melodic lines, anthemic build-ups, triumphant brass/synth fanfares, driving percussion.
7. **Nostalgic**: Tape saturation, vintage synth presets (Juno/Rhodes), bittersweet minor/major 7th shifts.
8. **Dark**: Tritones, harmonic minor or Phrygian mode, detuned synthesizers, sub-bass rumbles.
9. **Aggressive**: Heavy distortion, punchy transients, fast rhythmic attacks, pushed vocal delivery.
10. **Peaceful**: Gentle acoustic textures, long release times, minimal percussion, warm acoustic reverberation.
11. **Mysterious**: Dorian or Whole Tone scales, unexpected harmonic modulations, shimmering bells, reverse reverbs.
12. **Spiritual**: Drone harmonies, choral backing, meditative repetition, resonant bells or sitar.
13. **Cinematic**: Orchestral swells, dynamic volume range, Taiko drums, hybrid synth-brass horns.
14. **Emotional**: Dramatic dynamic builds from whispered verses to crying, belt-out choruses.

## 4. Purpose Classification

Music is frequently written for functional real-world contexts:

1. **Dance Music**: Relentless four-on-the-floor or syncopated groove, clear build-up and drop structure.
2. **Party Music**: Upbeat, sing-along chorus hooks, infectious rhythm, celebratory themes.
3. **Workout Music**: High BPM (130-150), driving repetitive pulse, motivational vocal cues, aggressive bass.
4. **Love Songs**: Intimate storytelling, acoustic instruments, lyrical tenderness, slow-to-mid tempo.
5. **Lullabies**: Low tempo (60-75 BPM), gentle acoustic guitar or music box, soft vocal delivery, no harsh drums.
6. **Devotional Songs**: Sacred themes, call-and-response vocal structures, reverent instrumentation.
7. **Wedding Songs**: Romantic lyrics, soaring string arrangements, joyful celebratory climaxes.
8. **Festival Music**: Massive outdoor sub-bass, chantable chorus hooks, explosive drops.
9. **Protest Songs**: Sharp narrative lyrics, acoustic or raw punk instrumentation, direct call-to-action hooks.
10. **Storytelling Songs**: Rich verse narrative, folk/country/hip-hop traditions, evolving characters.
11. **Advertisement Jingles**: Short (15-30 seconds), hyper-memorable hook, punchy brand-aligned rhythm.
12. **Background Music**: Unobtrusive volume dynamics, smooth textures, lack of distracting vocal lines.

## 5. Multi-Dimensional Composition

To construct a truly distinctive song, combine across all 5 dimensions:

$$\text{Song Identity} = \text{Genre} + \text{Mood} + \text{Purpose} + \text{Instrumentation} + \text{Vocal Style}$$

### Concrete Examples:

- **Formula A**: `Dark Phonk` + `Brazilian Funk` + `Aggressive` + `Night Drive / Workout` + `Distorted 808 Cowbell & Baile Bounce` + `Grimy Vocal Chants`
- **Formula B**: `Indie Rock` + `Nostalgic` + `Storytelling` + `Jangle Telecaster Guitar & Vintage Drums` + `Intimate Melancholic Baritone`
- **Formula C**: `Bollywood Pop` + `Celebratory / Romantic` + `Wedding Dance` + `Dholak, Sitar, Strings & Modern Kick` + `Soaring High-Tenor Duet`
- **Formula D**: `Synth-Pop` + `Heartbroken` + `Late-Night Walking` + `Juno-106 Synth Bass & Gated Reverb Snare` + `Falsetto with Slapback Echo`
